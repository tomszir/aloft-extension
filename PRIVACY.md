# Privacy policy

Aloft is a command bar for your browser. To find what you're looking for, it reads your open tabs, history, bookmarks and recently closed tabs **on your device, only while it's open**.

- **Nothing leaves your browser.** Aloft makes no network requests of its own. It has no servers, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't sell or share data with anyone.
- **Your searches stay local.** What you type is only sent anywhere when you choose "Search the web", and then it goes to your browser's default search engine, the same as typing in the address bar. With a keyword (such as `yt` for YouTube), it goes to that keyword's site instead, as part of the address Aloft opens, so it's saved in your history like any page.
- **What you pick is remembered on this device only.** So a page you keep choosing for the same text can move to the top, Aloft keeps what you typed and the page you picked in the browser's local storage. It's never synced, fades after a few weeks, and can be turned off or cleared in settings.
- **Web pages can't see it.** The command bar runs in an extension frame that the page you're on can't read, and Aloft never puts your tabs, history or bookmarks into a page.
- **Settings sync with your browser.** Your Aloft settings, themes and keywords are stored with the browser's own sync storage, so they follow your browser profile if you've turned on sync. They contain no browsing data.
- **Site icons come from your browser.** Favicons are read from the browser's own icon cache, so no site is contacted to show them. Firefox has no such cache an extension can read, so there Aloft shows each site's first letter instead.

Every permission Aloft asks for, and why, is listed in [docs/permissions.md](docs/permissions.md).

Questions or concerns: open an issue at <https://github.com/tomszir/aloft-extension/issues>.
